How Application Congestion Control Treats Transfers

In online banking architectures, financial transactions (money transfers, card authorization) are assigned Priority 1 over read-only synthetic queries (balance checks, mini-statements).

  • Normal Traffic: Immediate execution (~40-70ms) with zero queue wait.
  • Moderate Congestion: Admitted into priority queue, bypassing low-priority inquiries.
  • Severe Congestion: Low-priority requests are load-shed/dropped by the rate limiter, giving your transfer server resources.
